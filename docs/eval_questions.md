# Eval Questions

Use these questions to test the pipeline.

1. What are the main people mentioned in the documents?
2. Which organizations appear most frequently?
3. What are the strongest connections between the key entities?
4. Which document discusses the most important change or event?
5. What topics are repeatedly referenced across the corpus?
6. Which facts are strongly supported by several sources?
7. Which entity is most central to the memory graph?
8. What is the relationship between the main actor and the main institution?
9. What are the most likely conflict or disagreement signals in the source material?
10. What facts are uncertain or under-supported?
11. Which topics appear in only one document?
12. Which concepts appear to be newly introduced in the latest source?
13. What evidence supports a claim about a specific change event?
14. What are the most likely missing links in the graph?
15. What question would be difficult for a vector-only system but easier with graph-aware retrieval?
